Privacy policy

How personal information submitted through this website is handled.

Information we collect

We may collect names, contact details, journey details, flight information, passenger requirements and messages that you submit when requesting a quotation or making a booking.

How it is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, administer bookings, communicate about journeys, meet legal obligations and protect the business and passengers.

Retention and sharing

Information is retained only as reasonably required for enquiries, bookings, accounting, licensing, insurance and legal purposes. Relevant journey information may be shared with drivers or service providers involved in fulfilling a booking.

Your rights

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Add the business’s legal entity, postal contact address and data-controller details before publishing this policy.

This starter policy must be reviewed and completed with the business’s actual legal name, address, data practices, booking systems and retention periods before launch.
