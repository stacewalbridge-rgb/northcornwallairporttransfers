Airport travel guide

Bristol or Exeter Airport from North Cornwall?

Bristol often provides a wider choice of routes, while Exeter can offer a shorter and simpler ground journey for parts of North Cornwall. The best choice depends on the flight, total travel time and cost.

Compare the full journey, not only the airfare

A cheaper flight may involve a longer transfer, difficult departure time or additional overnight accommodation.

Check flight frequency

More frequent routes can provide better recovery options if a flight is disrupted.

Consider baggage and family needs

A direct flight at a better time may be worth more than the lowest headline ticket price.

Request transfer quotations before choosing

Knowing the ground-transport cost can help compare the true total cost of each airport.
